## LLM Distilled Operational Summary
### Operational Techniques & Decision Workflows
*   **Service Selection Workflow**: Evaluate current TDM exposure and bandwidth requirements against specific use cases (e.g., private line replacement, data center connectivity, or meshed multi-site networking) to select the appropriate Carrier Ethernet Network (CEN) service.
*   **Topology Design**: Choose between Point-to-Point (P2P) and Hub & Spoke configurations for E-Line EVPL services based on endpoint connectivity needs.
*   **Technology Migration**: Transition from legacy Time Division Multiplexing (TDM) to Ethernet over MPLS, SONET, or DWDM to leverage converged infrastructure and improved performance levels.
*   **Agility Implementation**: Leverage virtualized port configurations to match the speed of other IT stack services, reducing installation intervals (targeting ~15 business days vs. market average of 75).

### Critical Command Patterns & Required Flags
*   **Service Provisioning**: Utilize MEF-standardized frameworks to ensure interoperability and assured service levels across the Carrier Ethernet Network.
*   **Port Configuration**: Apply specific Class of Service (CoS) settings (Basic, Enhanced, Dedicated, or Premium tiers) to match transparency and performance requirements.
*   **Billing Configuration**: Select between Flat Rate (Nationwide/Metro/Intercity), Variable (by mileage), or Commit Plus Usage models based on the chosen service type.

### Network Topology & Relationships
*   **Network Architecture**: The CEN connects Subscriber Sites (UNI-C, UNI-N) via Operator Networks (OVC, EVC) using standardized interfaces like ENNI-N.
*   **Service Types & Speeds**:
    *   **E-Access/E-Line EVPL**: 2Mbps–6Gbps over MPLS/Layer 2; supports P2P and Hub & Spoke.
    *   **Ethernet Private Line**: 3Mbps–1Gbps over SONET, DWDM, or MPLS; offers full transparency.
    *   **VPLS**: 1Mbps–10Gbps over MPLS/Layer 2; ideal for data center connections and non-meshed large sites.
    *   **MPLS/IP VPN**: 1Mbps–10Gbps over Layer 3; designed for meshed, multi-site Layer 3 private networks.
    *   **Wavelengths**: 1Gbps–100Gbps (Ethernet) or 2.5/10/40Gbps (SONET/SDH) over DWDM for high-bandwidth ring services.
*   **Access Options**: Services support On-net and Off-net Ethernet configurations with varying transparency levels (Layer 2 to Full Layer 2 Transparency).
