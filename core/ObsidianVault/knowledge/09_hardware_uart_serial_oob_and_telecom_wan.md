# Domain Hub 09: Hardware UART Serial OOB (tio /dev/ttyUSB0 & COM1), Metro Ethernet, MPLS/GMPLS, OTN, PON/GPON, SD-WAN, Wi-Fi & 2G/3G/4G/5G/SDR Cellular

> **Summary:** Non-interactive tio 3.9 Lua --script UART serial console automation (/dev/ttyUSB0, -o 15 -O 120 pacing), Windows COM port OOB access, Carrier Metro Ethernet (MEF E-Line/E-LAN/E-Tree, 802.1ad Q-in-Q), MPLS/GMPLS label switching, Optical Transport Network (OTN/DWDM), PON/GPON fiber, SD-WAN overlays, 802.11 Wi-Fi, and 2G/3G/4G/5G/LTE/GSM/UMTS RAN & SDR analysis.  
> **When to Apply:** Apply whenever network connections to an appliance fail (`Exit 7` Connection Refused or `Exit 28` Timeout) and a hardware serial console (`/dev/ttyUSB0` or `COM1`) is available (`C1_OOB_SERIAL_LOOPBACK_APPLIANCE`), or when analyzing Carrier Metro Ethernet, MPLS/GMPLS labels, OTN/GPON optical links, SD-WAN tunnels, Wi-Fi 802.11, or 2G/3G/4G/5G cellular RAN protocols.  
> **Target OS Platform:** `cross-platform` | **Primary H-MDP Tactic:** `OOB_Serial_Access` (`s_l2_l3_verified -> s_oob_serial_enumerated`)  
> **Bayesian Root-Cause Alignment (`Engine 4`):** `service_bound_to_loopback, host_firewall_drop, wan_mpls_or_vlan_encapsulation_mismatch, uart_fifo_overrun_without_pacing`  
> **Network Isolation Compatibility:** `RED, BLUE (Local USB UART `/dev/ttyUSB*` and `COM*` ports are permitted in RED and BLUE LAN+USB modes)`  
> **Backlinks:** [[00_MEMORY_INDEX]] | [[00_TRAINED_INDEX]] | [[commands/windows_master_command_playbook]] | [[commands/linux_master_command_playbook]]
